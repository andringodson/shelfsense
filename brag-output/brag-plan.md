# ShelfSense: brag plan

**What:** a stock-out early-warning dashboard for quick-commerce dark stores, computed in SQL inside Exasol Personal.
**For:** dark-store and city ops teams. It tells them which shelves will run empty before the 06:00 truck, and what to do about each one.
**Sets it apart:** it sees stock-outs that sales data hides, replays any hour live, and backtests its own calls.
**Hook:** "An empty shelf looks exactly like a quiet hour."
**Tone:** polished (serious, elegant; soft fades, long holds). The palette and fonts are the product's own: forest #0F5C43, saffron #C96A0A, Instrument Serif and Inter.
**Share caption:** ShelfSense spots the empty shelf before your customer does: every dark-store shelf, re-scored hourly in Exasol.
